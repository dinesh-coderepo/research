# Claims Skeleton

> Use this as a starting point when drafting your own first-pass claims to brief the attorney. Replace bracketed `[…]` placeholders.

---

## Claim 1 — Method (independent)

> A computer-implemented method for [HIGH-LEVEL GOAL], comprising:
>
>   receiving, at [COMPONENT], [INPUT];
>
>   determining, by [COMPONENT], [INTERMEDIATE RESULT] based at least in part on [SIGNAL];
>
>   selecting [DECISION] that satisfies [CONSTRAINT]; and
>
>   [OUTPUT STEP], wherein [KEY DIFFERENTIATOR].

## Claim 2–5 — Method dependents

> 2. The method of claim 1, wherein [DETAIL ABOUT THE INTERMEDIATE RESULT].
> 3. The method of claim 1, wherein [ALTERNATIVE EMBODIMENT].
> 4. The method of claim 1, wherein the [SIGNAL] is derived from [HISTORICAL DATA / ML MODEL / HEURISTIC].
> 5. The method of claim 1, further comprising [OPTIONAL POST-PROCESSING STEP].

## Claim 6 — System (independent)

> A system comprising:
>   one or more processors;
>   a memory storing instructions that, when executed by the one or more processors, cause the system to perform operations comprising:
>     [REPEAT STEPS FROM CLAIM 1].

## Claim 7–10 — System dependents

> 7. The system of claim 6, wherein the system is hosted on a cloud platform.
> 8. The system of claim 6, wherein [HARDWARE-SPECIFIC EMBODIMENT].
> 9. The system of claim 6, wherein the operations further comprise [OPTIONAL].
> 10. The system of claim 6, wherein the [INTERMEDIATE RESULT] is determined by a machine-learning model trained on [DATA].

## Claim 11 — CRM (independent)

> A non-transitory computer-readable medium storing instructions that, when executed by one or more processors, cause the one or more processors to perform operations comprising:
>   [REPEAT STEPS FROM CLAIM 1].

## Claim 12–15 — CRM dependents

> *(mirror dependents 2–5 above)*
